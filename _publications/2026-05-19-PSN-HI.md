The existing health indicators (HIs) are commonly available under stationary conditions. They may not accurately represent the degradation trajectory and cannot be applied to incipient fault detection (IFD) and remaining useful life (RUL) prediction under non-stationary conditions. Therefore, a new polynomial speed normalized HI (PSN-HI) is proposed to enable both IFD and RUL prediction of variable-speed wind turbine (WT) bearings. Firstly, the speed effect elimination method based on the correlation between the energy ratio of each decomposed component and speed is presented to partially eliminate the effects of speed on monitoring data. Then, a polynomial speed normalization method is proposed using the genetic algorithm and preset objective function to normalize the reconstructed data and further eliminate the influence of speed on vibration data. On this basis, the time-domain and frequency-domain HIs are generated through two mixture models. The WT bearing HI is constructed by linear weighting the two HIs. Finally, the comparative experiment results indicate that PSN-HI better characterizes the degradation process quantitatively and achieves both IFD and RUL prediction under varying speeds compared to the classical and state-of-the-art HIs.
