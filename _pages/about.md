- I am currently a lecturer in the School of Mechanical Engineering at Chongqing University of Technology.
- I received my Ph.D. Degree in the School of Mechanical Engineering at Chongqing University, advised by Prof. Yi Qin.

Research Interests
======
My research interests lie in the general area of machine learning, particularly in mixture model and deep learning, as well as their applications in health indicator construction, remaining useful life prediction, and condition monitoring.
